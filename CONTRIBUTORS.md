Thank you to everyone who has helped build the XO License. Contributors are recognized individually, and their contributions remain theirs.

## How Recognition Works

- Contributors are listed here and, optionally, in `XO-ORIGIN.json` under `contributors`.
- Entries are added when a contribution is accepted.
- Entries are never removed without the contributor's request.
