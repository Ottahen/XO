# Code of Conduct

XO License is a community built on freedom and recognition. Everyone taking part should feel respected.

## Our Standards

**Encouraged**
- Respectful, constructive feedback
- Giving credit generously
- Welcoming newcomers
- Assuming good faith and asking questions

**Not acceptable**
- Harassment, insults or personal attacks
- Discrimination of any kind
- Publishing others' private information
- Taking credit for others' work
- Disruptive or bad-faith behavior

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions and any public space where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Report concerns through the project's issue tracker or by contacting the maintainer at https://github.com/Ottahen. Reports will be handled promptly and fairly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, contributions or participants that violate this Code of Conduct. Decisions may include a warning, a temporary restriction or a permanent ban, depending on severity.
